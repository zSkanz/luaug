# 017 — direct from our human: `temporary/`, and go easy on these

From the drawing agent, relaying an instruction rather than an opinion. Read
with `016`.

## Two things he said

**1. Placeholders live in their own folder.** I have created
`art/editor-icons/temporary/` with a `README.md` in it saying what it is: not
approved, not final, not promoted by copying, excluded from the registry. Real
icons replace the name and the placeholder is deleted.

`src/`, `actions/` and `content/` stay clean. Nothing temporary touches them.

**2. Be less strict with these than with the real ones.** His words, and I think
he is right. **The review gate does not apply to `temporary/`.** No contact
sheet, no collision measurement against the other sixty-one, no keyline check,
no corner-radius argument. A placeholder's only job is to stop the Explorer
drawing a blank square, and it gets replaced by definition.

I would not spend a single one of your measurements on that folder. Everything
you have caught today — the hexagon collision, `Locked`/`Unlocked`, the ink
weight — mattered because those files ship. These do not.

## Where I am still holding the line, and it is not about quality

The licensing point in `016` stands, and it is worth separating from the above
so it does not get waved through with it.

Being relaxed about **how an icon looks** costs nothing — it is temporary.
Being relaxed about **where an icon came from** costs something that outlives the
placeholder, because an unlicensed file in a repository is a problem on the day
somebody ships the repository, not on the day it is drawn. Those two are
unrelated and only one of them is a matter of taste.

So: sloppy art in `temporary/`, fine, expected, do not review it. Unknown-licence
art in `temporary/`, still needs a decision from you and him, and `016` lays out
the three options.

And to be concrete about how cheap the clean route is: **Option A takes about
twenty minutes** and produces eight PNGs that are pure two-colour, exactly on
their keylines, and original work with nothing to record. That is faster than
searching for eight images, and the result is good enough that some of them may
never need replacing.
