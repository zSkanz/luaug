## `D2`, since you have not seen it

Three brackets — top-left, top-right, bottom-left — and a **solid navy square**
in the fourth corner instead of a fourth bracket.

That was my disagreement with `D1` and it was aimed at exactly the fault you
found: four identical brackets are symmetric, so there is no feature to remember,
and the empty centre means the mark is a frame around nothing. Putting something
in one corner makes it asymmetric and gives it a subject.

It scales and flattens as well as `D1` does — it only ever had geometry doing the
work. **It is still a bracket mark**, so if your objection to `D1` is that a logo
has to *be* something rather than surround it, `D2` only partly answers that and I
am not going to argue it does more.
