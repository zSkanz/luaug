# 051 — Redraw `Locked` and `Unlocked`, both in ONE chat, locked first

From the reviewer. Our human looked at the pair beside each other and said the
`Unlocked` is wrong. He is right, and the measurement is worse than "a bit off".

## They are two padlocks, not one padlock in two states

Measured on the masters:

```
                Locked   Unlocked    delta
body width         745        603      -19%
body height        470        507      +37 px
body centre x      627        715      +7% of the frame
thinnest stroke     18         59      +228%
```

The body **shrinks, grows taller and slides right**. That is what the eye catches
the instant they are seen together, and no amount of getting the shackle right
fixes it — the subject changed between the two drawings.

This is `023`'s note arriving as a number. I wrote then that the body had to be
`Locked`'s body unchanged in every dimension. It is not, and the reason it is not
is that they were drawn in two separate sittings.

## The method our human asked for, and it is the one that just worked

**Both in the same chat. `Locked` first, then `Unlocked` as a follow-up in that
same conversation, referring back to it.**

This is exactly what produced the stamp badge an hour ago: base first, then the
mark as a follow-up, and the two came back with **zero pixels of difference** on
the outer silhouette. Same technique, same reason — a follow-up in a live chat
has the previous image as context, and a fresh chat has a sentence.

## The two turns

**Turn 1** — Part A unedited, then:

> a **solid padlock, closed**. A wide rounded-square body filling the lower two
> thirds of the keyline, with a shackle above it: a thick arch, both ends landing
> on the top edge of the body, enclosing a clear hole. One keyhole cut from the
> body — a circle with a short tapering slot below it — centred left to right and
> sitting slightly below the body's middle.

**Turn 2** — in the same chat, no Part A again, just:

> Now the same padlock **open**. **The body is identical** to the image you just
> drew — same width, same height, same corner radius, same position on the
> canvas, same keyhole in the same place. Change **only the shackle**: its right
> foot stays landed on the body exactly where it was, and the left side lifts
> clear so its end floats above the body's top-left corner with a visible gap.
> The shackle keeps the same stroke thickness it had. Nothing else moves.

## What I will measure when they land

The same three numbers, and the bar is not "close":

```
body width        within 2%
body centre x     within 1% of the frame
shackle stroke    within 10%
```

If the body moves, it is not the pair — send the second turn again in the same
chat rather than starting over, because the chat is the thing doing the work.

## Why this matters more than one icon

A toggle is the only place in a set where two icons are **the same object**. Every
other pair only has to be different. This is the one that has to be the same, and
it is why it has failed twice: the instruction "make them match" is not a drawing
instruction, and "here is the one you just drew, change one thing" is.

Filed for the brief either way. **When two icons are one object, they come from
one chat.**
