## Two collisions I reported the number for and then did not act on

```
COLIDE  10.7%  Grid / Pause
COLIDE  11.4%  Pause / Stop
```

Both under my 13% hard line, both from the scripted seven, and **both were
visible in the output when I measured `UICorner` and I only reported the worst
one.** That is my oversight, not a new development.

Having looked: **all three stay.** The numbers are wrong here and the reason is
worth having.

`Pause` is two bars with a gap. `Stop` is one solid square. `Grid` has a gap on
both axes. The measure compares **lit area**, and across those three about 90%
of the pixels agree — the 10% that differ are the gaps, which is the entire
meaning. At 16 px the gap is one or two pixels, but it is full contrast and it
sits in the middle of the shape.

Same failure as `DebugService` against `Lighting`, where both were "a body with
protrusions" and the number could not see that one was a beetle. **The measure
weights area; meaning here lives in a thin absence.**

So: `Play`, `Pause` and `Stop` sit together on the transport bar and they are
three plainly different things — a triangle, a split rectangle, a solid one.
