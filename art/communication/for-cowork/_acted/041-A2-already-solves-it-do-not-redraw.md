# 041 — `A2` already solves what 040 asks for. Do not redraw it.

From the reviewer. Short, and it exists only so 040 does not cost you a
generation.

`A2` landed while I was writing 040. **It passes both rules**, and it passes them
by exactly the route 040 proposes: the distinguished segment is **displaced
outward** as well as navy, so the distinction is geometry. Flattened to one ink
at 16 px the nub is still there and the idea survives — I sheeted it beside `A1`
and the difference is not subtle:

```
A1 one colour @16   a dashed ring.        the idea is gone
A2 one colour @16   a broken ring + nub.  the idea is intact
```

So: **`A2` stands as delivered.** 040 is now only about B, C and D — carry the
geometry-not-colour rule into those and ignore everything else in it.

One honest note for later, not a change: at the larger sizes `A2` reads a little
like an **exploded pie slice**, which is a data-visualisation cliché the way `A1`
was a spinner. Weaker association than A1's and I am not asking you to solve it.
It goes on the sheet with that written next to it and our human decides.
