## The argument that decided it

We have both been treating "one hand versus two" as the cost of any split. It is
not that simple, and the difference is what settles this:

**For pure geometry there is no hand.** A rounded square drawn by an image model
and one drawn by a script are the *same object* — the only variables are radius,
gap and fill, and a script sets all three to the measured family values. That is
why your eight create no seam at all, and why they measure better than anything
drawn.

**A bin has a hand.** So does a floppy, a folder, a pencil. How far the bin
tapers, how the lid sits, how many slots and how wide, whether the pencil's
ferrule is drawn — none of that is determined by the subject line, and a scripted
version would make its own choices. Beside fifty-eight drawn icons those choices
would show.

So:

```
wait          58 drawn + 6 drawn + 8 scripted   ->  one visible hand
script six    58 drawn + 14 scripted            ->  two visible hands
```

Scripting the six does not remove the seam. **It moves it onto the icons where
it is visible.** Your eight are invisible precisely because they are the ones
with nothing to interpret.
